Package price and AC upgrade details
  • The ₹5,499 starting price applies to the base non-AC package and is subject to confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the ₹5,499 starting price?
The base package uses non-AC bus travel, a non-AC room and a non-AC sightseeing vehicle. Confirm the final itinerary and inclusions with us before booking.
How much do AC upgrades cost?
The additional charge is ₹500 per person for each selected upgrade: bus, room or sightseeing vehicle, subject to availability and confirmation.
Is the displayed price guaranteed for all dates?
No. Starting prices are subject to dates, group size, availability and the final confirmed quotation.
How do I book a package?
Send an enquiry on WhatsApp. Confirm availability, itinerary, payment terms and cancellation conditions before making a booking payment.
